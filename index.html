Data Projects

🔱 Is UCSD Walkable? Mapping Campus Walkability

Is UCSD Walkable? is a scrollytelling data story exploring walking distances across UC San Diego's eight colleges, built with Leaflet, D3.js, and Scrollama, revealing how your college determines your relationship with everything on campus.

🌊 ENSOcast: Decoding El Niño–Southern Oscillation

Presented at the San Diego Undergraduate Tech Conference 2025, ENSOcast predicts monthly ENSO phases using Random Forest, XGBoost, 1D CNN, and LSTM models trained on decades of NOAA data.

GitHub View Project →

🔥 America on Fire: Mapping U.S. Fire Distribution

An interactive data visualization of U.S. wildfires in 2024 built with D3.js and NASA MODIS data, highlighting spatial patterns, seasonality, and unexpected hotspots.
